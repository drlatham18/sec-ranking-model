How the model was built

The model predicts each team’s SP+ overall rating — an opponent-adjusted points-per-game figure — from information available before the season starts. Candidate inputs cover prior on-field results, recruiting, roster talent, returning production, the transfer portal, coaching continuity and conference context.

Nothing is weighted by opinion. Ridge regression estimates every coefficient. Features and the winning configuration are selected on walk-forward development seasons, then frozen before evaluation on the untouched 2023–2025 holdout.

How flags work

There is no minimum edge, liquidity, or momentum requirement. Thin liquidity, wide spreads, missing fees, and price movement appear as notes. We still match both teams and kickoff time and exclude started games, props, and invalid quotes so comparisons refer to the same event.

The good-buy exit is a scenario, not a trained price forecast. Momentum does not establish that odds will keep moving. The gain includes estimated entry and exit taker fees, but future liquidity and slippage are unknown. A team can appear in both categories. No orders are placed by this page.
