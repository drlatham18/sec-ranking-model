# Data and product notice

Hedges & Margins produces independently derived forecasts from historical
college-football data obtained through CollegeFootballData.com. Raw API
responses in `data/raw/` are private build inputs and must not be distributed
as part of the product or a public source archive.

Team and conference names identify factual subjects of analysis. This project
is not affiliated with or endorsed by the NCAA, the SEC, ESPN, any school,
conference, sportsbook, or data provider.

Forecasts are probabilistic estimates for informational and entertainment
purposes. They are not guarantees or financial advice. If betting-related
features are added later, obtain jurisdiction-specific legal review and add
appropriate responsible-gambling controls before launch.
